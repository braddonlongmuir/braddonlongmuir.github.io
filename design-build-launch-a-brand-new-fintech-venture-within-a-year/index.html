Design, Build & Launch a Brand New Fintech Venture Within a Year

Lumiant

Experience Designer | 2019 - 2021

How do you build an entirely new fintech venture in less than a year? Our team designed Lumiant, a financial advice platform built around a simple idea: financial planning should start with what matters to people, not just their money.

Challenge

Traditional financial advice can feel technical and transactional. Lumiant wanted to help advisers have more meaningful conversations with clients and connect financial strategies to their personal values and life goals.

The challenge was turning that idea into a distinctive brand, a usable digital product and a cohesive experience within a tight launch timeframe.

Approach

As part of the Tobias team, I worked across product, brand and communications design, helping translate the venture's concept into tangible experiences. I designed finance and reporting concepts, exploring how financial information could be presented clearly and connected to clients' broader goals.

Beyond the digital product, I contributed to Lumiant's brand identity, including logo design and brand guidelines. I worked on marketing and advertising collateral to keep the visual language consistent across different touchpoints, and created promotional video production and motion design deliverables.

Working across these disciplines meant thinking about the experience as a whole. The product, brand and marketing all needed to tell the same story: a different kind of financial advice, centred on people and the lives they wanted to lead.

Outcome

Lumiant launched within nine months, bringing together a values-centred advice proposition, digital tools and a cohesive brand experience. Product design is more than interface. Together product, brand and story make a new idea easier to understand, trust and ultimately adopt.

The project was awarded a 2021 Gold Good Design Award for Commercial Services ↗︎.
